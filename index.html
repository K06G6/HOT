Who We Are

We Build Conditions For Culture To Exist

House of Thunder is a multidisciplinary cultural hub and non-profit organization supporting artists, creators, musicians, filmmakers, technologists, researchers, designers, and experimental thinkers.

We create spaces where projects can grow through collaboration, community, mentorship, production support, exhibitions, events, and shared resources.

We believe culture does not happen in isolation. It happens when people build together.

"I don't predict the future. I find the people that are building it and make sure nothing stands in their way. Ni retirada ni rendición, ahúa!"
— Bryan Jimenez, CPO
